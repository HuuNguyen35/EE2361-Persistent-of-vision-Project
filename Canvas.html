Circular Pixel Art Generator

Polar-grid “LEDs” for POV-style blades. Click or drag to paint.
Geometry
Number of concentric rings from center to edge. Suggested: 14 for text.
Even numbers only. Suggested: 24 for text with variable-width 3×5 font.
Relative to spacing 70%
Adjust to match your physical LED diameter vs spacing. Circles stay uniform across the grid.
Color & Painting
Hex: #ff0077 RGB(255, 0, 119)
Brush mode
Single = one LED. Fill ring = all LEDs in that ring. Fill radius = all LEDs along that angle. Dragging works in all modes.
Recent colors
Click a swatch to reuse a color. Stores the last 3 colors you actually painted with.
Sets all LEDs to the current color (background), then you can paint on top.
Preset patterns
Patterns adapt to your current LED count and radii.
Pattern colors
Pick 2–4 colors. The pattern cycles through them; unused inputs are optional.
Overwrites the current canvas with the pattern. Use Undo if you change your mind.
Text
Variable-width 3×5 pixel font (1–3 columns per character) with a 1-column gap between characters. Best with 14 radial pixels & ~24 columns.
